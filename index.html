01 — Overview

A defined rebuild scope inside an established Pacific Palisades condominium community.

Vista Del Mar Townhomes — originally developed as Headland Condominiums — is an established coastal community off Palisades Drive. The reconstruction scope covers 17 total-loss units at 1900–1932 Palisades Drive, lost in the January 2025 fire.

The remaining 8 units at 1934–1948 Palisades Drive survived intact and sit outside the primary reconstruction scope. The result is a contained, well-documented rebuild within a recognized footprint rather than raw ground-up development from a blank site.
