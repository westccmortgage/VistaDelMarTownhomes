01 — Overview

An established community with a defined reconstruction scope.

Vista Del Mar Townhomes, originally developed as Headland Condominiums, is an existing residential community in Pacific Palisades. Seventeen residences at 1900–1932 Palisades Drive were total losses in the January 2025 fire.

Eight additional residences at 1934–1948 Palisades Drive remain intact and are outside the primary rebuild scope. The project is therefore a reconstruction within an existing community rather than a new subdivision created from a blank site.
