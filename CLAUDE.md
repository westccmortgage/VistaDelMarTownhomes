# Vista Del Mar Townhomes — Repository Instructions

## Canonical site
- Domain: https://vistadelmartownhomes.com
- Repository: westccmortgage/VistaDelMarTownhomes
- Production branch: main
- Purpose: public informational project website only.

## Site role
Vista Del Mar Townhomes is the factual, public project-information site for the Pacific Palisades community rebuild. It is not the investor website and must not become a capital solicitation page.

## Content rules
Keep:
- project history and January 2025 fire-loss context;
- 17 fire-loss units and 8 intact units;
- site / cluster organization;
- original unit mix and historical plan information;
- reconstruction concept;
- diligence / planning status;
- approved conceptual rendering, clearly labeled as conceptual.

Do not publish here:
- financial models;
- construction budgets or sponsor internal cost;
- projected revenue or resale pricing;
- investor equity;
- construction-loan terms;
- preferred returns, IRR, equity multiples, waterfalls or profit splits;
- fundraising language, urgency or guaranteed-return language;
- seller agreements, title/payoff/insurance files or other private diligence.

Capital-facing information belongs on Pegasus Lenders Group, not this site.

## Accuracy
- Distinguish original/historical areas from final planned areas.
- Do not state that townhome sizes will remain unchanged. Final reconstructed areas are under design review and may change subject to approvals.
- Do not state final schedules, permits, costs or approvals unless verified.
- Existing eight units remain outside the primary 17-unit rebuild scope.
- Preserve approved media; do not generate replacement project renders without approval.

## Deployment
Netlify should connect this repository and deploy from main.
